Every dive at the Catalinas turns up something different, from whitetip reef sharks resting on the sand to eagle rays gliding past in the current.

Sea turtles browsing the reef, moray eels tucked into the rocks, and schools of jacks moving as one. Pufferfish, octopus and seahorses reward the divers who take the time to look closer at the volcanic rock itself. Two private guided dives at one of Costa Rica's most iconic dive destinations.

Yes. This fun dive is for certified divers only. If you're not yet certified, try our Discover Scuba Diving experience or take a PADI Open Water course.

The full trip runs from about 6:45 AM to 12:00 PM, including the boat ride (around 40 minutes each way) and two dives at the Catalina Islands.

Whitetip reef sharks, eagle rays, sea turtles, moray eels, schools of jacks, pufferfish, octopus and seahorses, and, from December to May, giant manta rays.
