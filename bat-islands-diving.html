The Bat Islands

Advanced Diving

One of the few places on Earth with regular bull shark encounters

The Bat Islands (Islas Murciélago) sit off Santa Rosa National Park, within the Marine Sector of SINAC's Área de Conservación Guanacaste, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. A study published in the Revista de Biología Tropical documented a high residency index for bull sharks at the San Pedrillo Islet dive site here, confirming it as an important aggregation site for the species.

Bull Sharks, Respectfully

Unbaited, natural, and closely supervised

Encounters here are completely unbaited. The bull sharks gather naturally at a cleaning station, where smaller fish remove parasites and dead skin from their bodies. It's a behavior you get to watch play out at close range. Diving with large sharks always calls for caution and strict safety procedures, but no shark-related incidents have been recorded at this dive site.

Built for Experienced Divers

Reserved for Advanced Open Water divers

The depth and current conditions at the Bat Islands put this dive a step beyond our other sites, which is why it's reserved for certified Advanced Open Water divers. Not there yet? The Catalina Islands suit a wider range of levels, and our PADI Advanced Open Water course can get you ready for the Bat Islands on the same trip.
