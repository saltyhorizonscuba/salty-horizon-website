Our Story

Two divers, one shared passion.

Two divers, one route

Meet your instructors, then follow the oceans that took us to Tamarindo.

Our story began far from Costa Rica, in Australia, where we first met through our shared love for the ocean and adventure.

Since then, diving has taken us around the world, from the Caribbean and Pacific to Indonesia, Australia, Europe, and the waters of Costa Rica. We travelled, worked, trained, taught, explored, and learned from different diving communities and marine environments.

Those experiences shaped the way we see diving today. For us, diving has never been simply about checking a dive site off a list. It is about slowing down, paying attention, learning something new, and creating a genuine connection with the ocean.

PADI OWSI & MSDT

Camille

Camille discovered scuba diving at 14, and it quickly became much more than a hobby. Her curiosity for the underwater world led her to pursue further certifications, completing her Divemaster training in 2020 and becoming a dive instructor in 2021.

Read more

Her passion for diving has always been closely connected to marine conservation, particularly the protection of sharks. Over the years, she has participated in conservation projects in Costa Rica and abroad, including reef mapping expeditions in the Cayman Islands and shark conservation expeditions to Isla del Coco with Misión Tiburón.

In Indonesia, she spent 1.5 years as a full time dive instructor in Nusa Lembongan, where she certified hundreds of divers while also leading the ecology department. She introduced ecology focused courses and used diving as a way to help divers better understand and respect the marine environment.

Her work has also taken her beyond teaching. In 2024, she worked as a dive photographer on the Great Barrier Reef, combining her passion for exploration with her love for documenting the underwater world.

For Camille, teaching diving is ultimately about sharing more than a certification. It is about creating awareness, confidence, curiosity, and a deeper connection with the ocean.

PADI OWSI

Julien

Diving has always been part of Julien's life. Coming from a family of dive instructors in France and Guadeloupe, he was introduced to the underwater world from an early age. At 13, he took his first breath underwater and quickly discovered a passion that would stay with him.

Read more

His journey eventually took him to Menorca, where he completed his Divemaster training and gained experience in the many sides of working in diving. From guiding and assisting divers to learning the importance of preparation, safety, and teamwork, these experiences shaped his approach to the profession.

Julien became a PADI Instructor in 2025, driven by a genuine passion for sharing knowledge. For him, teaching is about more than mastering skills. It is about helping people feel comfortable, confident, and curious in the water, while discovering the ocean at their own pace.

And then came Salty Horizon.

  • Personal.
  • Flexible.
  • Thoughtful.

After years of travelling, diving, teaching, and working in different parts of the world, we found ourselves back in Costa Rica.

We realized we wanted to create something of our own: a diving experience that reflected the way we believe diving should be.

Rather than a traditional dive center, Salty Horizon was created around the idea that every diver is different.

Some people are discovering the ocean for the first time. Others have hundreds of dives but want to explore somewhere new. Some want to learn, some want to slow down, and some simply want to spend a day on the water with the people they love.

So we created Salty Horizon to make diving more personal, with private experiences, small groups, flexible itineraries, and a genuine focus on the people behind every dive.

Today, we bring together everything we have learned along the way: our experience as instructors, our love for exploration, our respect for the ocean, and our belief that the best underwater experiences are the ones that stay with you long after you surface.

This is Salty Horizon.
