Who we are

Diving done the right way

We are Camille & Julien, two PADI instructors who turned a lifelong love of the ocean into a different kind of dive experience.

Camille grew up between Costa Rica's jungle and its coast and started diving at 14. Julien comes from a family of dive instructors in France and Guadeloupe and took his first breath underwater at 13. Neither of us really had a choice: the ocean was always going to be part of the plan.

What we didn't plan was how far it would take us. Before settling in Tamarindo we lived, travelled and dived in Spain, Indonesia and Australia. Every one of those places taught us something about the sea, and about the kind of dive center we'd want to be guests of ourselves.

That's Salty Horizon: small, fully private groups, in English, French or Spanish, at your pace. No rushed schedules and no crowded boats, just the two of us and our full attention.
