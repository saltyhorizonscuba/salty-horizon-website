One of the few places on Earth with regular bull shark encounters, completely unbaited and closely supervised.

The bull sharks gather naturally at a cleaning station, where smaller fish remove parasites from their bodies, a behavior you get to watch play out at close range. Three privately guided dives at one of Costa Rica's most memorable dive sites.
